    How does this work?

    There is no single public API that gives current serving status for a USNA class. We start with public graduation and commissioning releases. For 2012, only the documented Navy/Marine commissioning subtotal is modeled; the other 22 graduates' commissioning dispositions are unresolved, not assumed to be U.S. commissions or zero other-service commissions. International graduates and non-commissioning graduates should be excluded when a full breakdown is established.

    The intended evidence pipeline uses Navy NAVADMIN and Marine Corps MARADMIN officer promotion results (O-4/LCDR/major and above), matched to a verified commissioning year and academy cohort. A recent promotion is evidence of service at that time, but lists include non-USNA officers, omit non-selectees and some components, and cannot by themselves measure everyone still serving. A missing name does not mean someone left.

    The initial 2012 model has not performed those individual matches. Instead, it applies documented, illustrative service-specific retention assumptions to documented commissioning counts. MyNavy HR publishes promotion messages, but they do not establish the current USNA-class denominator or rank distribution. Broad DoD officer continuation statistics are context only: annual continuation, retention at five years, and retention at fourteen years are different measures. We do not present one as another or claim these assumptions are published cohort rates.

    “Still in” is intended to include active duty and reserve service in any U.S. service; public promotion evidence does not yet establish that coverage. The residual “out” category can include other losses, including deaths, and cannot distinguish separation from retirement. Estimates are rounded by service, then summed. The as-of date is the model's reference date, not a personnel-record update.

    Voluntary reports are separate evidence. Rank percentages describe only respondents who report still serving, including “Not disclosed”; they cannot be extrapolated to the entire class. Maintainers manually check for duplicate/update submissions, replace prior accepted responses, and update the single CSV's aggregate rows. No report is an independently verified personnel record.

    Only aggregate counts are published on the site and in its CSV. Individual identification is not the goal, and no names appear here. Voluntary GitHub issues and usernames are public, as disclosed before submission. Adding a class means adding cited rows to the CSV and rebuilding the generated class index. See the repository for inputs and the Python builder.

    This is an unofficial estimate built from public sources and could be wrong or out of date. Not affiliated with or endorsed by the U.S. Naval Academy, the Department of the Navy, or the DoD.
